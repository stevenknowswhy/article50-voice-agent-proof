## What this proves—and what it does not

The recording demonstrates the intended words and order for disclosure, consent, bounded qualification, sensitive-data suppression, and human handoff. It does not prove carrier connectivity, inbound number routing, outbound permission, latency, interruption handling, transcription accuracy, recording controls, or production deployment.
